**ZENNY**

**Overview for Design Team**

*A plain-language brief for social, web, and marketing design work — not a technical spec.*

# **1\. What Is Zenny?**

Zenny is an AI-powered customer agent that businesses put on their website, chat widget, or socials. It talks to customers directly — answers questions, books appointments, takes orders, follows up on abandoned carts/leads, and hands off to a human when needed.

**Think of it as:** a smart, always-on employee who knows the business inside out and treats customers like people, not tickets.

It's built by ZeroManual, and it's not a generic chatbot — it's designed around customer psychology (trust-first, no pressure, no fake urgency).

# **2\. Core Features (in plain terms)**

* **24/7 availability** — never sleeps, never puts anyone on hold

* **Chat \+ Voice** — works as text chat or spoken conversation (phone/web call)

* **Instant answers** — pulls from the business's own knowledge (policies, products, FAQs)

* **Books & completes actions** — appointments, orders, reservations, sign-ups, directly in conversation, no forms needed

* **Remembers context** — doesn't make customers repeat themselves

* **Follows up naturally** — gentle reminders for abandoned carts, missed bookings, unanswered questions (not spammy)

* **Knows its limits** — hands off to a real human for anything sensitive, complex, or high-stakes

* **Fits any business** — works for shops, clinics, service providers, agencies, nonprofits, etc.

# **3\. How It Helps Clients**

(the business owners buying Zenny)

* Never lose a customer to “nobody picked up the phone”

* Cut down repetitive questions their staff has to answer

* Recover sales/leads that would otherwise be abandoned

* Give customers a faster, calmer experience than being passed around

* Free up human staff for the conversations that actually need a human

*One-line pitch: “Zenny makes sure no customer ever gets ignored.”*

# **4\. Personality & Tone**

(for how Zenny should “feel” visually and in copy)

## **Zenny should come across as:**

* Calm, competent, warm — like a helpful person, not a robot script

* Confident but not pushy — no fake countdowns, no “only 2 left\!” urgency

* Clear and human — short, plain sentences over corporate jargon

* Honest about being AI — doesn't pretend to be human, isn't shy about it either

## **Consider avoiding:**

* A “salesy” bot that pressures people into buying

* Overly cute/gimmicky mascot-like character (can feel untrustworthy for business use)

* Cold/robotic/sterile tech imagery — it should feel approachable

# **5\. Visual Direction — Suggestions for Storyboarding**

## **Good directions to explore:**

* Real-feeling conversation snippets (chat bubbles) showing Zenny solving a problem smoothly

* Before/after: customer stuck waiting → customer helped instantly by Zenny

* Zenny shown inside real contexts — a website corner widget, a phone screen, a laptop — rather than an abstract standalone “robot” character

* Clean, modern, friendly UI mockups (matches a real product, not sci-fi AI clichés)

## **Directions to be cautious with:**

* Overly futuristic/sci-fi robot imagery — sets wrong expectation (this isn't a novelty gadget)

* Heavy use of glowing neural-network/brain graphics — overused AI cliché, feels generic

* Making Zenny look fully human (photoreal human face claiming to be the agent) — misleading

* Cluttered screens with too much text — the product's value is simplicity

# **6\. Integrations & Dashboard**

## **Where Zenny connects (common channels):**

| Channel | Why it matters |
| :---- | :---- |
| Website chat widget | The core, always-on entry point |
| WhatsApp / Instagram / Messenger | Meets customers on social/DM |
| SMS / text | Simple, no-app-needed follow-ups |
| Email | Confirmations, recovery messages |
| Voice (phone calls) | Spoken conversation, not just typing |
| Calendar sync | Books directly into the client's existing calendar (keep generic — no specific brand) |

*Visual idea: show Zenny as one hub connecting to these familiar channel icons, rather than a single-app screenshot — communicates “meets customers wherever they are.”*

## **Dashboard (accountability & visibility):**

Clients get a dashboard where they can see exactly what Zenny is doing — conversations handled, bookings made, leads recovered, performance over time. This matters for trust: business owners aren't handing off blindly, they can always check in and see the results.

*Visual idea: simple, clean dashboard mockup — a few key stats/cards (conversations handled, bookings made, response time), not a cluttered admin panel. Feels like “peace of mind,” not “complex software.”*

# **7\. Suggested Post Style (social/marketing)**

Tone: short, confident, benefit-first. Lead with the outcome, not the tech.

## **Formats that tend to work well:**

* **Split-screen “without Zenny vs. with Zenny”** — left side shows the old pain (unanswered phone, “we'll get back to you,” a customer giving up), right side shows Zenny resolving the same moment instantly. Works as a static image, carousel, or short before/after video. Keep the “without” side relatable, not exaggerated — the contrast should feel true, not mocking.

* **Conversation-snippet screenshots** — a realistic chat bubble exchange (2–4 messages) showing Zenny solving one clear problem: answering a product question, confirming a booking, recovering an abandoned cart. Best as a single focused moment. Works well as a static post, carousel card, or a looping short video showing messages appear in real time.

* **Stat-style callouts** — one bold number or short claim per post (“Answers in seconds. Any hour.”). Best paired with a clean background and minimal text — this format is for scroll-stopping, not explaining. Good for Stories, quick social cards, or ad creative.

* **Testimonial-style quotes** — a business-owner voice describing the outcome in their own words (“I stopped losing customers to missed calls”). Pair with a simple, non-generic portrait or business-context image. Works well as a quote card or short testimonial-style video.

* **Day-in-the-life moments** — short narrative post or reel showing a real-world moment (customer messages late at night, gets an instant helpful reply, books something). Humanizes the “24/7” feature without just stating it as a fact.

* **Dashboard/proof-of-work posts** — a glimpse of the dashboard paired with a caption like “See exactly what Zenny's doing for your business” — reinforces trust and transparency as a selling point, not just a feature.

## **Demo post content examples (for reference/inspiration):**

*“Your customers don't wait for business hours. Neither does Zenny.”*

*“Missed calls \= missed sales. Zenny answers every time.”*

Chat mockup example:
